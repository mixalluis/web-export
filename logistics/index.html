Logistics, Loading Ports & Shipping Terms

Clear Incoterms, container stuffing capacities, and standard export documentation to ensure frictionless customs clearance in your country.

Supported Incoterms (ICC 2020)

FOB (Free On Board)
Port of Tanjung Perak (IDTPE) / Tanjung Priok (IDTPP)

We handle inland transport, export customs, and loading onto your nominated vessel. Risk passes to buyer once loaded.

Cost & Risk: Seller clears export; buyer procures ocean vessel freight & marine insurance.
CFR (Cost and Freight)
Any worldwide named destination container port

We arrange and pay ocean freight to your named port of destination. Buyer assumes risk from loading port and procures marine cargo insurance.

Cost & Risk: Seller pays ocean shipping freight; buyer procures destination clearance.
CIF (Cost, Insurance & Freight)
Any worldwide named destination container port

We cover freight plus comprehensive marine cargo insurance policy directly to your destination seaport.

Cost & Risk: Seller pays ocean freight and marine cargo insurance policy (Institute Clauses A).

Mandatory Export Documentation Suite
