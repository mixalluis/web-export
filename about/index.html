About PT Anurika Nusantara Agro

Connecting Indonesian Smallholders to the Global Marketplace

Established in Surabaya, Indonesia, we bridge the gap between conscientious local farming cooperatives and demanding overseas importers.

Ethical Sourcing with Rigorous QC

Many foreign buyers struggle with unreliable intermediaries in Indonesia—facing inconsistent moisture, adulterated grades, or surprise shipping delays. We built our operation to eliminate that risk.

By maintaining direct relationships with smallholder farmer collectives and running rigorous batch inspections, we ensure grade purity, legal export compliance, and dependable container fulfillment.

Centralized QC & Export Warehouse · East Java Facility (Access to Tanjung Perak Port)
